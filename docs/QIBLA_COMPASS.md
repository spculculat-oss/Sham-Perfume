# بوصلة القبلة جنب الساعة / Qibla compass

1. في الرئيسية، جنب الحاسبة، اضغط **حدّد القبلة بموقعي**. لو مش عايز تسمح بالموقع، **اختار مدينة** قريبة منك؛ ده تقدير من وسط المدينة، مش من مكان الموبايل بالضبط.
2. اضغط **فعّل البوصلة** واسمح بالحساسات لو المتصفح طلب. امسك الموبايل أفقي بعيد عن الحديد والمغناطيس، ولفّه لحد ما سهم القبلة يقابل العلامة فوق. رسمة الكعبة بتتمايل في وسط القرص. تقدر توقف البوصلة من نفس المكان.
3. لو السهم ثابت ومكتوب **الشمال فوق**، دي خريطة اتجاه، مش قراءة لاتجاه الموبايل. الرقم هو الزاوية مع اتجاه عقارب الساعة من الشمال الجغرافي. لو المعاينة ما سمحتش بالحساسات أو الموقع، افتح الصفحة في تبويب مستقل على الموبايل. في باقي أقسام الموقع البوصلة موجودة جوه **أدوات الساعة**.

## Reference and fallbacks

- `qiblaAt` computes the initial great-circle bearing to the Kaaba (21.422487° N, 39.826206° E). It uses the device fix at its original resolution, not rounded weather coordinates. It declines to show a bearing if the position is too imprecise, at either pole, or too close to the Kaaba or its antipode for a reliable angle. The user can request a new one-shot fix when they move; the page does not track position in the background.
- Live direction needs a north-referenced `deviceorientationabsolute` event (or `deviceorientation` with `absolute: true`) or a valid `webkitCompassHeading`. [The Earth orientation frame](https://www.w3.org/TR/orientation-sensor/#earths-reference-coordinate-system) and [WebKit’s iOS heading implementation](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/blob/main/Source/WebCore/platform/ios/WebCoreMotionManager.mm) reference magnetic north. We do not use a relative gyroscope angle or GPS course as north. The displayed screen top is projected onto horizontal ground, including in landscape. Hardware, OS calibration, browser implementation and nearby interference affect accuracy.
- A bundled [WMM2025](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-magnetic-model) model, via `magvar` ([MIT notice](../public/third-party/magvar.txt)), supplies east-positive declination at the selected point. `true heading = magnetic heading + declination`. The Qibla pointer uses `true bearing − true heading`. In magnetic caution/blackout areas (horizontal intensity below 6,000 nT) or dates outside 2025–2029 the map remains north-up. So does a missing, unavailable, or stale heading. Some browsers send readings only when the phone turns; after 30 seconds without a new reading, turn the phone slightly to refresh. Listeners stop in hidden tabs, on Stop, and when the compass closes.
- Sensor and location buttons are separate, and neither asks for permission automatically. Qibla doesn't send the device fix to a server or persist it or its derived angle. A browser's geolocation permission may be shared across the site, but weather requests a device fix only through its own location button. Without a selected location the regular compass still displays a magnetic heading when available. Reduced-motion mode keeps the Kaaba steady and disables easing, without suppressing actual compass updates.

## Verification

- Unit math and NOAA test vectors: `node --import tsx --test tests/qibla.test.ts tests/visitorCompass.test.ts`.
- With the site running, `CLOCK_BASE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:3000/ BROWSER_EXECUTABLE=/path/to/chromium node scripts/check-visitor-compass.mjs` exercises simulated location and orientation events in the rendered app. This is not a handset calibration test.
- On a sensor-equipped Android phone and iPhone, open the HTTPS page in its own tab. From Cairo the north-up diagram should show roughly 136°. Enable the compass, hold it flat, and compare the north mark and direction with a calibrated compass referenced to geographic north. Turn slowly past north, rotate the display to landscape, try denying each permission separately, and leave/re-open Clock tools. A phone without a valid north reading should never show a live Qibla arrow. Check near the Kaaba against a known local direction, not a city-centre estimate.
